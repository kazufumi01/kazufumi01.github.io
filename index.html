Why these labs

See the inside, not just the knobs

Gain reduction, transfer curves, harmonic spectra and signal flow update as you play, so cause and effect are visible.

Fair listening

Level matching, bypass and delta listening are built in, so you judge the process — not just loudness.

Real numbers

Every parameter comes with typical settings and the formula behind it, written for students, not marketing.

Free for classrooms

No account, no install. Open the link on any laptop and teach with it.
