Support

Keep the labs free.

Every lab and plug-in here is free, with no ads and no sign-up, and I plan to keep it that way for students and teachers. If one of them helped your mix or your class, a small tip pays for the time it takes to build, test and translate the next one.

How to support

One-time tip, any amount, via PayPal (card payments work too):

Other ways that help just as much: share a lab with a friend or your class, report bugs on GitHub, or listen to my music on Spotify.

ミックスや授業で役に立ったら、PayPal から金額自由の投げ銭で開発を支えていただけるとうれしいです（カード払いも可）。
