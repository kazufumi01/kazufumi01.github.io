About this page

A study note that takes apart a real compressor DSP running in your browser, one parameter at a time. Headphones recommended. Even without sound, the graphs and “What the sound is doing” show the effect.

No sound? On iPhone / iPad, turn off silent mode, raise the volume, then press Play. Space toggles play / stop.

← All labs Previous version 日本語

The DSP on this page is an educational implementation. The VCA, FET, opto and vari-mu modes are simplified models of each element’s behaviour and do not reproduce any specific hardware or plugin. Models of classic units are covered separately (as in the Compressor Anatomy Lab 2 plug-in).

Reference: D. Giannoulis, M. Massberg, J. D. Reiss, "Digital Dynamic Range Compressor Design — A Tutorial and Analysis," J. Audio Eng. Soc., vol. 60, no. 6, 2012.
